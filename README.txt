MoneyMap Personal Budget Website

1. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
2. Click "Upload statement" and select a CSV export from your bank or credit card.
3. Supported fields include Date, Description/Merchant, Amount, Debit, Credit, Type, and Category.
4. Your data remains in your browser using localStorage. This prototype does not send files to a server.
5. Review auto-categorized items before making financial decisions.

For website hosting, upload index.html to your web host. Because statement data is sensitive, do not add server-side uploads without authentication, encryption, access controls, retention policies, and security testing.

6. Tax Years view groups imported expense transactions by calendar tax year and category. Use the year and category filters to drill down.

7. Expense Management lets you filter expenses, mark them Personal/Business/Mixed, review status, flag potential tax expenses, add notes, and reclassify categories.
